Contour measurements

Trace the lesion border to calculate.
— — — pointer from the patient (marked in the app) · ——— your line.
On a laptop: two fingers on the trackpad move the image · pinch (or Ctrl + two fingers) to zoom.
With a mouse: the wheel zooms at the cursor.
✋ Move image lets you drag with a normal click — and the cursor becomes a hand.

Trace in pieces if you need to — the line continues from where it stopped. Then click ✓ Close and create nodes: the contour becomes small squares that you drag to fit the border. How many you moved is recorded.

The patient's pointer cannot be edited: a wrong pointer is clinical information, not an error to clean up. It can be hidden with the eye button.

The outline belongs to the physician, not the machine — the program only turns the line into numbers. Without a calibrated marker it shows proportions, never invented millimetres.
